tfalgo Quant Workstation — Interactive User Guide

Hover or tap any ⓘ icon across the app for field-level help.
1. Portfolio & Smart Execution: Enter a % Capital Invest (e.g. 5%), Limit Price, and Stop Loss (₹ or %). Clicking Place Smart Order + Auto-SL sends a LIMIT order, chases LTP every 5s (up to your Settings retry limit), and arms an SL-M + optional partial Target Limit orders automatically.
2. 10s SL Watchdog & Telegram Alerts: Every 10 seconds the background worker syncs with your broker, verifies your SL order is still open (re-placing or Market-exiting if removed/breached), and evaluates your Price Alerts to ping Telegram remotely.
3. Pre-Trade R:R & Kelly Calculator: Computes exact shares, Risk %, Breakeven Win Rate, Expectancy (₹ & R), and Kelly compounding trades needed before you place a trade.
4. Trade Journal & Screenshots: Click any Open Position or Closed Trade to view every fill, add setup notes, upload chart images, or press Ctrl+V to paste clipboard screenshots directly.
5. Scanners & Backtest Studio: Scan stored stocks and click Load Trade → to pre-fill the Portfolio ticket. Use Backtest to test dynamic_v1 / ema_breakout_v1, upload CSV/Excel/Parquet data, and run Monte Carlo drawdown stress tests.
6. Settings (.env, PyOTP, MongoDB): Configure Paper, Zerodha, Fyers, or Kotak Neo credentials in .env, set up PyOTP 2FA, test multi-bot Telegram alerts, and connect MongoDB for permanent trade & audit persistence.
